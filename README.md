# 🎯 AVTrack - Track people in complex video scenes

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-AVTrack-blue.svg)](https://github.com/marie-jeannesotho844/AVTrack/releases)

AVTrack uses sound and sight to follow people in busy videos. It keeps track of individuals even when they walk behind objects or into crowds. Researchers built this tool to help computers understand human movement in real-world settings.

## 📋 What this tool does

AVTrack connects audio data with video images. This helps the software maintain focus on a person even if the video quality drops or the scene gets crowded. It works well in places like stadiums, parks, or stores. The software identifies humans and draws a path for each person it finds. It keeps this path consistent throughout the entire video clip.

## 💻 System requirements

Your computer needs to meet these basic standards to run the software.

*   Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).

## 🎥 How to track your first video

The interface includes a simple workspace for your tasks. 

1. Open the AVTrack application from your desktop.
2. Click the "Open Video" button at the top left corner of the window.
3. Select any video file from your computer. Note that short clips process faster than long movies.
4. Click the "Start Tracking" button.
5. Watch the progress bar at the bottom. The software analyzes the audio and the visual frames.
6. Once the process completes, the software displays the video with paths drawn over the people.

## ⚙️ Adjusting your view

You can change how the software shows the information. 

*   **Toggle Paths:** Turn the lines on or off to see the original video clearly.
*   **Audio Focus:** Use the slider to prioritize audio cues if your video contains loud background noise.
*   **Confidence Threshold:** Adjust this if the software loses track of people too often. Higher settings force the program to pick only the clearest targets. Lower settings capture more people but might confuse them when they cross paths.

## 📁 Saving your results

AVTrack saves your work automatically. You can export the tracking data to a text file for use in other programs. Click "File," choose "Export Data," and pick a save location on your computer. This creates a file containing the location of every person in every frame of your video.

## ❓ Troubleshooting common issues

If the software does not work as expected, check these common fixes.

*   **Software crashes on start:** Check that your graphics card drivers are current. Visit your computer manufacturer's website to download the latest updates.
*   **Slow processing:** Close other programs like web browsers or games while you run AVTrack. These programs use the same memory as our tracker.
*   **Video format not recognized:** AVTrack reads most common video files. If your file does not load, try converting it to an MP4 format using a standard video converter tool.
*   **Black screen:** This happens if the software cannot access your graphics hardware. Ensure your monitor plugs directly into your dedicated graphics card and not the motherboard.

## 📖 Frequently asked questions

**Does the software upload my videos?**
No. AVTrack runs entirely on your computer. Your videos and tracking data stay on your hard drive.

**Can I track multiple people at once?**
Yes. The software recognizes different individuals simultaneously. It assigns a unique color and ID number to each person.

**Does it require an internet connection?**
No. The software functions fine without an internet connection.

**Does it work with live video feeds?**
This version works only with saved video files. 

**Is this software free?**
Yes. The project follows an open-source model. You may use it for personal or academic purposes. 

**How do I uninstall the program?**
Open your Windows Settings, go to Apps, find AVTrack in the list, and select Uninstall. This removes the program from your machine.
